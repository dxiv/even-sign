How it works (G2 controls)

The G2 uses a left list (swipe to move, tap to run). After you Send or pick a phrase: Prev · Next · Replay · Clear · Phrases · < Exit. Double-tap list or ring/temple goes back one level; double-tap again on the idle home list opens exit. Double-tap empty space on this page (away from buttons and the text field) for the same exit prompt, or use Exit app at the bottom.
Shortcut: Ctrl+Enter sends. After Send, Alt+←/→ steps the preview.

Aid only — not a certified interpreter or emergency service. Text stays here until you send. Speech may use your device recognizer.
